06 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Visual instinct.
Editorial thinking.

I’m Murali M — a Director of Photography and Video Editor with a postgraduate background in Journalism and Mass Communication.

My work brings together camera craft, editing and visual communication. I have worked on institutional productions, short films, education campaigns, event coverage and photography, while also contributing to practical media environments through studio and production workflows.

Research remains an important part of my practice. It gives me another way to examine media, audience behaviour and the impact of visual communication — connecting creative decisions with a wider understanding of society.
