05 / ABOUT

A visual storyteller with a researcher's curiosity.

I’m Murali M, a Director of Photography and Video Editor with an academic background in Journalism and Mass Communication. My work sits at the intersection of visual craft and communication — understanding what a story needs, finding the right way to capture it, and shaping it carefully in post-production.

My experience includes institutional video production, short films, education campaigns, event coverage, photography and media-related work. Alongside professional practice, I continue to explore research questions around digital media, learning behaviour, journalism and credibility.

I value thoughtful preparation, clear collaboration and work that communicates with purpose.
